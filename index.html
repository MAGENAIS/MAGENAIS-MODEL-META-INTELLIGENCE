Meta-Intelligencemagenais.meta-intelligence · v2.0.0 · experimental

Plans and records how a task goes from problem to outcome.

A stateful orchestrator that records strategies, alternatives, their evaluation, a composition decision, an execution plan, the claimed outcome and an adaptation decision, with the origin of every item. It never executes anything: plans and decisions are data, and the caller does the real work.

Overview

What V2 does

The V1 pipeline tracks a task through ten forward-only stages: intake(), understand(), addGoalsConstraints(), addEpistemicTracking(), decomposeCapabilities(), generateCandidateStrategies(), evaluateStrategies(), governAction(), recordResult() and recordAdaptation(). V2 adds an additive layer that does not change those: generateStrategies() and generateStrategyAlternatives() derive real strategies from your capability graph; evaluateStrategyOptions() ranks them with the bundled DecisionScore algorithm and reports stability (DSI) and flip points (DFP); authorizeComposition() records an ACT/WAIT/ASK/SIMULATE decision; buildExecutionPlan() derives a plan only for ACT; recordExecutionOutcome() and recordExecutionAdaptation() record and mechanically verify the outcome; getProblemRepresentation() and getCognitiveTrace() give derived, read-only views. Invalid calls throw one of 43 typed errors and leave the task unchanged.

Status

Implemented, validated, not implemented

  • Implemented and tested: the V1 pipeline, all V2 methods above, 43 typed errors, the default and custom scorers, the manifest, a 51-case benchmark and 9 guard ablations.
  • Validated: automated tests, strict type checking, manifest schema validation and nine runnable examples (see the repository for the current results).
  • Not implemented by design: executing, composing or retrying anything; inventing goals, evidence or strategies; calibrated probabilities; persistence.

Know before you use it

Limitations

  • It records and plans; it does not act. An ACT decision or execution plan proves nothing happened.
  • verified-against-basis means a goals/constraints-and-knowns basis is recorded, not that a claimed success was independently confirmed.
  • Governance, composition and adaptation decisions come from the caller; no independent check of their soundness is made.
  • Strategy generation is minimal and limited to what your capability graph reports.
  • There are no probabilities or confidence percentages; uncertainty is structural (DSI, DFP, origins, abstention).
  • State is in memory only. The benchmark uses synthetic fixtures and is a regression check, not evidence of real-world performance.
  • Not a routed MAGENAIS model: it is used in-process as a library and is not registered in the Models Hub registry. The Models Hub lists it as an information-only entry with no Run panel.
