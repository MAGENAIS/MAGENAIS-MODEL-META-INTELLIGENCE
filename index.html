Meta-Intelligencemagenais.meta-intelligence · v0.1.0 · work-in-progress

Orchestration layer for MAGENAIS's cognitive pipeline.

Advances a caller's problem through a fixed sequence of stages — intake, understanding, goals/constraints, and known/unknown/assumption/evidence tracking — recording every stage's output alongside, never in place of, what came before, with each item tagged as caller-stated or Meta-Intelligence-extracted.

Overview

What it does

Given a caller's problem statement, Meta-Intelligence tracks a task through a fixed pipeline of eleven stages, of which the first four are implemented in v0.1.0: intake() records the statement verbatim; understand() derives a mechanically whitespace-normalized restatement; addGoalsConstraints() records goals and constraints; and addEpistemicTracking() records knowns, unknowns, assumptions, and evidence. Each stage can only run once per task, in order — calling a stage out of order or a second time is rejected rather than silently re-run.

Every goal, constraint, known, unknown, assumption, and evidence item is tagged with its origin: 'caller' for text supplied verbatim by the caller, or 'extracted' for text Meta-Intelligence derived. The two are never merged or silently rewritten into each other, even when their text happens to coincide. It is a research-oriented, early-stage orchestrator — not yet a Model the MAGENAIS router can call. See docs/roadmap.md for the full pipeline and what's still to come.

Meta-Intelligence tracks provenance for every item it records; it does not itself judge whether a goal, assumption, or piece of evidence is correct.

Know before you use it

Limitations

  • No capability decomposition, strategy generation/selection, execution, verification, or adaptation yet — only intake(), understand(), addGoalsConstraints(), and addEpistemicTracking() exist to call.
  • Each pipeline stage can only run once per task, in the fixed order above — calling a stage out of order or a second time throws rather than silently re-running it.
  • Item text (goals, constraints, knowns, unknowns, assumptions, evidence) is kept verbatim, only trimmed — Meta-Intelligence never infers, rewrites, or deduplicates across items or origins.
  • Not yet wired into a MAGENAIS ModelHub/ModelRegistry — this repository and the copy under MAGENAIS-main's src/ModelsHub/meta-intelligence/ are kept in sync by hand.
